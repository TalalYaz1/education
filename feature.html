OUR APPROACH

The right person is about more than a CV.

Schools need people who are dependable, professional and appropriate for the environment they are working in.

That is why we take a school-focused approach to education staffing. We aim to understand the role, the setting and the support required before identifying suitable staff.

From safeguarding and compliance through to communication and ongoing support, our approach is designed around creating reliable staffing partnerships with schools.

WHAT GUIDES US

Four principles behind our approach.

Our approach is built around the things that matter most when supporting schools and pupils.

Safeguarding

Safeguarding sits at the centre of our recruitment and compliance approach.

Suitability

We focus on finding staff who are appropriate for the role, setting and requirements of the school.

Communication

Clear communication helps schools understand the staffing process and allows requirements to be handled efficiently.

Reliability

We aim to provide dependable staffing support that schools can build long-term relationships around.

SAFEGUARDING

Safeguarding is built into the process.

Working with children and young people carries significant responsibility. We believe safeguarding should be considered throughout the staffing journey rather than treated as a final administrative step.

Safer recruitment

Recruitment processes are designed with safeguarding and suitability in mind.

Compliance checks

Appropriate checks and documentation form part of our candidate compliance process.

Ongoing responsibility

Safeguarding remains an important consideration throughout our relationship with schools and staff.

SCHOOL PARTNERSHIPS

Working alongside your school.

We want our relationship with schools to go beyond simply providing a candidate.

Every school has different pupils, priorities, staffing structures and expectations. We therefore aim to understand each requirement individually rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach.

Whether you require planned support or need help responding to an unexpected staffing gap, our aim is to provide a clear and professional service.
