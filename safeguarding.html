Safeguarding Policy Statement

SwiftPath SEN Education is fully committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people.

Our Commitment

  • All staff undergo Enhanced DBS checks
  • Safer Recruitment procedures are followed at all times
  • All candidates receive safeguarding awareness guidance
  • We comply with Keeping Children Safe in Education (KCSIE)

Reporting Concerns

All safeguarding concerns are escalated immediately to the Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL).
