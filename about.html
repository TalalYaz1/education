WHO WE ARE

Education staffing with purpose behind it.

SwiftPath SEN Education was created with a simple purpose: to help schools access reliable support when they need it.

We provide education staffing solutions focused on teaching assistants, learning support practitioners and 1:1 classroom support for pupils with additional learning needs.

We recognise that every school, pupil and staffing requirement is different. That's why our approach is centred around understanding the needs of the setting before identifying suitable support.

Our aim is not simply to fill vacancies. It is to help schools find people who can contribute positively to their pupils, classrooms and wider education teams.

OUR MISSION

Helping create better learning environments.

Our work is built around the belief that the right support can help pupils engage, participate and thrive in education.

Pupil focused

We keep the needs of the pupil at the centre of the staffing process, recognising that effective support must be individual and appropriate.

Safeguarding led

School focused

OUR COMMITMENT

Safeguarding is at the heart of what we do.

Working within education carries significant responsibility. We believe safeguarding must be considered throughout the staffing journey, not treated as a final administrative step.

Our approach places importance on safer recruitment, appropriate checks, compliance and professional conduct when identifying staff for education settings.

Safer recruitment principles

Recruitment processes designed with safeguarding in mind.

Compliance focused

Appropriate documentation and checks form part of our staffing process.

Professional standards

We expect staff working through SwiftPath to uphold professional standards within education environments.
