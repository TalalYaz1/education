WHO WE ARE

Specialist staffing with purpose behind it.

SwiftPath SEN Education is a UK-based education staffing provider focused on connecting schools with reliable, professional support staff.

We specialise in teaching assistants, learning support practitioners and 1:1 classroom support for pupils with additional learning needs.

Our approach combines safeguarding, careful recruitment and responsive school support to help create positive learning environments for pupils and staff.
