About Us

SwiftPath SEN Education is a specialist education recruitment agency focused on supplying high-quality support staff to schools and educational settings across London and surrounding areas.


Our mission is simple: to connect schools with dependable, safeguarding-focused professionals who help pupils thrive.


We understand the importance of consistency, professionalism, and safeguarding within education and are committed to building long-term partnerships with schools and Multi-Academy Trusts.
